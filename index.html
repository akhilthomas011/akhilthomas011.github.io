01 About

Cloud consultant by profession, photographer by heart.

I'm a cloud consultant with a strong track record in the information technology and services industry, focused on Microsoft Azure — from infrastructure and identity to automation and infrastructure as code.

I hold the Microsoft Certified Solutions Associate (MCSA) and Microsoft Certified Professional (MCP) certifications and a B.Tech in Computer Science. I'm always looking for ways to eliminate repetitive work and speed up delivery through automation.

Away from the terminal, I'm out with a camera — mountains, mist and whatever the light is doing.
